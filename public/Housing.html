possible solutions

USE ONE'S WASTE AS YOUR RESSOURCE

In switzerland, the company Infomaniak has decided to use the heat generated by it's AI' data center Euria to heat homes. The same idea has been implemented in Finland. The goal is to make sure nothing is wasted while producing something.

A DECENT HOME FOR EVERYONE

Completed in 2023 in Son Servera, Majorca, Spain, we can find a social housing that is both pleasant and welcoming. It is a project that conveys an important message, as it is built in an area where property speculation is skyrocketing.
