SHARING AREAS

The main problems

The problems :

These days, many people have all kinds of items in their homes that they rarely use but feel they must keep because they are irreplaceable.

Possible Solutions

The problems :

In our city, we would like to create sharing zones where people can find these items and borrow them. This would prevent every resident from having to buy these items, which is beneficial from both an economic and environmental standpoint.
