BUS AND RAILWAY STATIONS

current problems

COST FOR TRAVELERS

Most public transports in France require a form of payement that often represents a costly investment. For instance, a train ticket between Paris and Lyon is around 50 €. A subway travel card for a year in paris can go from 928 to 998€.

UNRELIABILITY

Public transports are often seen as unreliable as they might be cancelled due to bad weather or any other unpredictable events. More than 21% of users have faced delays more than 30 minutes long.

AN UNSUITABLE FLOW

During rush hours, public transports are often packed, making them stressfull and unpleasant. Some travelers might even not be able to get in, delaying their commuting.

possible solutions

FREE PUBLIC TRANSPORT

A few cities in France have already implemented free public transports for everyone or a category of people, like Compiegne's buses (everyone) or Montpellier's public transports (students). In germany, the Deutschland-Ticket costs 63€ per month and lets its owner travel easily by all means of local public transport throughout Germany.

ECO-FRIENDLY VEHICULES

Clermont-Ferrand Metropole has implamented hydrogen-powered buses in its network, reducing noises, vibrations and emissions. Those 14 buses help prevent 600 tonnes of CO2 emissions per year. We can find similar initiatives in Pau, Dijon, Le Mans, etc.

MODERNISE THE RAIL NETWORK

Upgrading the network helps to minimise unforeseen issues and deal with them more effectively, making it more reliable. Furthermore, using more efficient materials, tools and trains makes the network more energy-efficient and sustainable as well as reach destinations faster.
