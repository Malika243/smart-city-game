SHOPS

current problems

BIG SHOPS ARE OUTSIDE THE CITY

Most supermarkets or big shops about DIY, sports, are located outside the city and can therefore be reached only by car or a long bus journey.

PRODUCTS FROM ABROAD

The food market is very competitive and it is often cheaper for companies to buy from outside of France or outside of Europe. However this doesn't benefit french's producers, the quality is often lower (less restrictions) and the transport has a strong carbon footprint

possible solutions

LOCAL SHOPS

If we want to promote a city where cars aren't needed, local shops are mandatory. We believe that having many small and specialised shops placed downtown and around housings is more convenient.

LOCAL PRODUCTS

If we want to reduce the financial and environmental costs of transporting goods, we must prioritise the sale of local and seasonal produce. That is why shops should, as a matter of priority, work in partnership with local producers.
