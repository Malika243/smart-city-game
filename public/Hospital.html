HOSPITAL

current problems

LACK OF WORKERS

Mainly due to poor working conditions and low wages. Because of it, 85% of CHU report having had to temporarily close beds due to staff shortages and 83% report being unable to maintain a fulfilling personal life

DETERIORATION IN ACCESS TO CARE

40% of French people feel their access to care has been reduced (Fédération Hospitalière de France) and 54% of French people fear they will not be able to access healthcare

SHORTAGE OF SUPPLIES

5,000 reports of stockouts, according to the National Agency for Medicines Safety. In May 2026, the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz sparked fears of a shortage of medical plastic products (syringes, catheters, masks) and tensions remain.

possible solutions

BETTER WORKING CONDITIONS

Improve working conditions and salaries for emergency room doctors in order to make the profession more attractive. Improve the funding for public hospitals to prevent all doctors from leaving for the private sector

RELIEVE HOSPITAL'S PRESSURE

Expand telemedicine services for cases where in-person visits are not necessary, in order to relieve pressure on hospitals and thereby improve access for both critically ill patients and those with less complex needs

PREVENTION

Step up prevention efforts to reduce the number of preventable cases
